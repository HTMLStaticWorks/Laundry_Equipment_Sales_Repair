TROUBLESHOOTING KNOWLEDGE

FREQUENTLY ASKED TECHNICAL QUESTIONS

Solutions to common operational symptoms, error codes, and utility connection questions.

What causes Error Code E-04 (Water Timeout)?

Error E-04 occurs when fill time exceeds 6 minutes. Check water inlet strainers for sediment buildup, verify inlet pressure is above 3 bar, and ensure chemical dosing signals are synchronized.

How often should washer bearing seals be greased?

For machines operating 10+ cycles daily, high-temperature synthetic grease should be injected into main bearing housings every 500 operating hours or during monthly maintenance checks.

What G-force extraction is recommended for hotel linens?

A minimum of 350 G to 400 G-force is recommended for hotel sheets and towels. High extraction reduces residual moisture content to under 45%, cutting drying fuel costs substantially.
