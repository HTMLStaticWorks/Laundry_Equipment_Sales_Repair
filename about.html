OUR COMPANY & PHILOSOPHY

OUR ORIGIN

BUILDING UNCOMPROMISING RELIABILITY

Founded in 2008, Laundry Equipment Sales & Repair was established with a singular focus: supplying commercial laundry facilities with heavy-grade machinery built to endure round-the-clock operational strain.

We believe that commercial laundry is the backbone of hospitality, healthcare, and retail laundromats. When a machine stops running, a business stops earning. That’s why every piece of equipment we sell is paired with complete OEM spare parts inventory and 24/7 technical repair dispatch.
